Close the shared overlay/field spine by deleting the last private copies of what it owns. Across tickets 36 to 45 the components migrated one family at a time; this change takes the last fourteen files — nine hoisted `const selfFocusRing = …` constants, five inline `focusRing({ target: "self" })` calls inside recipes, two per-render `within` calls, and the ten hoisted `state` rungs behind the react-aria quarantine. `styles/utils` now resolves all five fixed rungs once at module scope (`selfFocusRingClass`, `withinFocusRingClass`, `withinFocusRingControlClass`, `stateFocusRingClass`, `stateFocusRingVisibleClass`) and is the only module that calls `focusRing` with a fixed argument; `react-aria/link`, which passes a live `isFocusVisible` per render, is the single pinned exemption. Toast's popup fill comes from the shared `overlayPopupFillClass` rather than a fourth copy of the same two tokens. Checkbox, ConfirmButton, and the interim GridList drop their three copies of the same eight-line "is this `ReactNode` a string" helper for the shared `isTextNode`; the two guards in the packed-export check that evaded the anti-slop rule — one spelled `Object.prototype.toString.call(v) === "[object String]"`, the other `Object(target) === target` — collapse into one honest, named `typeof` check and its negation.

No public API change and no rendered-class change: the constants are the same expressions, resolved once per process instead of once per module, verified by comparing every affected component's rendered class attributes and every affected recipe's slot output before and after. `source-contracts.test.ts` now pins the five single-owner contracts the spine was built for — the focus-ring rungs, the `ThemeScope` portal guard, the popup class vocabulary, `createStringDictionary`, and `isTextNode` — so a private copy cannot grow back.

Size budgets are re-measured across the whole spine (tickets 29, 36–45). Ten entries came in under their recorded measurement and had their ceilings tightened by exactly the bytes lost: `card`, `input-group`, `checkbox-card`, `timeline-list`, `heading`, `react-aria/calendar`, `react-aria/range-calendar`, `react-aria/date-picker`, `react-aria/date-range-picker`, and `styles.css`. No ceiling was loosened.
