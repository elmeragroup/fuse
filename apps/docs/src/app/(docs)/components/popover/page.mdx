---
title: Popover
lede: >
  A non-modal overlay anchored to its trigger. `Popover.Content` owns placement and the
  portal target; `showArrow` is opt-in so the popup can sit flush or grow a tokenized
  arrow.
---

<ComponentIntro slug="popover" />

<Prose>

Positioner props (`side`, `align`, `sideOffset`, `alignOffset`) surface on
`Popover.Content`. Portal, Positioner and Popup stay unexported — `container` on Content
is the only portal-control surface, and it defaults to the nearest `ThemeScope`. The
arrow is off unless `showArrow` is set; that is deliberate and is not unified with
Tooltip.

</Prose>
