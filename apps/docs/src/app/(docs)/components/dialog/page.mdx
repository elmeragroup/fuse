---
title: Dialog
lede: >
  A modal overlay. `Dialog.Root` owns the open state, `Dialog.Content` sizes the popup
  and ships the corner close button, and the portal targets the nearest `ThemeScope`
  so overlays stay inside the theme that opened them.
---

<Prose>

`Dialog.Content`'s `showCloseButton` controls the corner dismiss affordance;
`Dialog.Footer`'s `showCloseButton` appends a labelled footer action. They are separate
jobs and both stay.
