## Composed charts

<Prose>

Bars and lines share one Root and one x axis. Write the parts in paint order, and every series reads one value axis.

To give two series of different units their own value axis, put each in a `Chart.Panel`. The Panels stack as rows on Root's x grid and share its surface, tooltip and table. Each Panel takes its own `yBaseline` and `yInclude`, and Root's own `yBaseline` and `yInclude` then govern nothing. Every config key sits in one Panel, and every plot part sits in the Panel whose series it draws. The x axis goes in the last Panel. Write config in Panel order, because Up and Down walk the series in config order across the Panels.

A series may take a coarser `interval` than Root's, such as hourly usage under quarter-hour prices. Its bar spans the quarters of its hour, and the option, the tooltip and the table name its hour. Fuse never sums or averages.

</Prose>
