<Prose>

Frame has no base-ui primitive and no recipe. Every part is a styled element, so the
compound stays server-safe. `Header` and `Footer` sit on the muted ground as siblings of
`Panel`, not inside it. `Title` is a `div` on purpose — frames nest at arbitrary depths,
so heading level is the consumer's call (wrap a heading as children).

`stackedPanels` is the only axis: adjacent panels either sit on a 4px muted gutter or
fuse into one card. The `frame-panel-*` slot prefix is the ref's selector contract, not a
typo — `Table` (and any `in-data-[slot=frame]:` consumer) keys off these values.

Card and Frame do not share props, variants, or imports. Reach for Card when the surface
stands alone; reach for Frame when panels group or stack on muted ground.

</Prose>
