<Prose>

The chart entry is a preview. Names and props can change until Fuse's first stable release. Root takes the reading in its own unit, with `min` and `max` around it, and names the dial as a meter: a screen reader reads its name, its domain and the value in Root's format. Nothing in the chart takes focus.

Every part draws from one value. The track scales out once, the first time the gauge comes into view, then the fill sweeps from the minimum to the reading, lighting each notch as it passes, while the ruler's ticks brighten and the needle turns with it. A new reading sweeps on from wherever the dial shows. With reduced motion, and in print, the dial draws at rest at once.

</Prose>

## Against a limit

A `Zone` marks a range at the rim, here the step above the 5 kWh capacity limit. The track scales in to make room for it, so no radius needs tuning.
