`layout="inline"` keeps each term and its details on one row at every width, for label/value rows in receipts, order summaries and price boxes. The details align to the end of the row. A short one never wraps, and a long one, such as an address, wraps within 60% of the row. Inline rows take the label type, so they follow density like field labels, are spaced by the small surface gap and have no rules. The term is not muted; add `text-muted-foreground` to a term to mute it, or `font-medium` to a details to emphasise it. To group rows, use one inline list per group: consecutive inline lists are spaced by the large surface gap. Several terms of one details stack in the term column, and the details sits on the row of the last one. Several details of one term stack in the details column.
