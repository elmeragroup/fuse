## Composition limits

`Root` renders exactly one scrollbar. For two-axis scrolling, mount two `ScrollArea.Bar`s yourself; the last demo shows the composition.

`Root` takes either a fixed height or a max height. Under a max height, short content keeps the area at its content height, and longer content scrolls once it reaches the cap.
