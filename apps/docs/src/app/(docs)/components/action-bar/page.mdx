  A toolbar of actions on a selection, such as the checked rows of a DataTable, that floats at the
  bottom of its container while anything is selected. Use it for bulk actions on lists and tables.

<Prose>

`ActionBar.Root` takes the selection's size as `count`. While it is above zero the bar rises into
place at the bottom of its nearest positioned ancestor, centred and 16px in from its edges; when it
returns to zero the bar falls away and unmounts. With reduced motion it only fades. Give the
container `position: relative` and keep the bar its last child, so it paints over the rows.

The bar is a [Toolbar](/components/toolbar): one Tab stop, with the arrow keys moving between its
items. It is named "Bulk actions" in the reader's locale unless you give it an `aria-label`. A
visually hidden live region beside it announces the count politely, "3 selected", each time it
changes, the first selection included.

`ActionBar.Count` shows that count. `ActionBar.Clear` is the X button named "Clear selection". It
clears nothing itself: empty the selection in its `onClick`. When the selection empties while focus
is in the bar, as after Clear, focus returns to the element it came from, such as a row checkbox. If
that element is gone, as after deleting the selected rows, focus falls to the page.

`ActionBar.Button` is an extra-small ghost Button in the bar's arrow-key order. Lead it with an icon
marked `data-icon="inline-start"` and put its words in `ActionBar.ButtonLabel`. While the
container is narrower than 28rem, the bar shows the icons alone, and the words stay as each
button's accessible name.

</Prose>
