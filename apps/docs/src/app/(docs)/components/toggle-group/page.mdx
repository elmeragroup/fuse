<Prose>

`ToggleGroup.Root` is a `role="group"` over the base-ui primitive. `multiple` defaults
to `false` — zero-or-one pressed, and clicking the pressed item clears the selection.
Set `multiple` for independent multi-press. `onValueChange` always receives an array of
pressed values.

Item-level `variant` and `size` win over the group's. Leave them unset on an item to
inherit; a standalone `ToggleGroup.Item` falls through to the `toggleVariants` defaults.
`spacing={0}` collapses items into one segmented control, with first/last caps restored
on the orientation axis. Icon-only items need `aria-label`.

The public `toggleVariants` recipe lives on `@elmeragroup/ui/toggle` — this entry does
not re-export it.

</Prose>
