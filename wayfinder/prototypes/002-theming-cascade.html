Theming cascade mechanism

The question: can 16 theme permutations (variant × brand × segment) be expressed with data-attribute selectors without a combinatorial explosion of CSS — and is the three-attribute form better than one slug attribute or the classes both reference apps use today? All three mechanisms below are generated from one shared value table by a small pure module, so they render identically; what differs is the CSS text, the selector semantics, and what stays free for future dark mode.
