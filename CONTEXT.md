# CONTEXT

Ubiquitous language for the `@elmeragroup/fuse` whitelabel component library effort.

## Glossary

- **Brand**: a visual-identity code used on `data-theme-brand` — the six consumer-facing energy brands plus corporate Elmera (`elma`), each with a fixed four-character code: Fjordkraft (`fkas`), TrøndelagKraft (`tkas`), Gudbrandsdal Energi (`guen`), Fjordkraft Företag (`fkab`), Fjordkraft Konsument (`fkse`), Nordic Green Energy (`ngfi`, Finland), Elmera (`elma`). Note: `fkse` renders under the consumer-facing trade name **Telinet** (logo and palette) while keeping the `fkse` code. Brands found in reference code but outside this theme set: Steddi, Trumf.
- **Segment**: the customer class a surface serves — `private` (B2C) or `company` (B2B).
- **Pinned brand**: a brand that exists in only one segment. `fkab` is pinned to `company`; `fkse` is pinned to `private`. The other five brands span both segments. `fkab` shares Fjordkraft's (`fkas`) visual identity by deliberate, permanent policy — it is an alias, not a missing palette. Each `fkab` theme resolves to the tokens of its `fkas-company` twin, segment sheets included; only the brand pointer names `--brand-fkab`.
- **Variant**: the audience axis of a theme — `internal` (grayscale theme for internal tools, brand appears only in accents/logos) or `external` (full brand look-and-feel for customer-facing apps).
- **Density**: a document-level axis that sets the density metrics, independent of theme. Legal values `dense` and `comfortable`, stamped as `data-density` on the document root. Variant supplies only the deployment default (`internal → dense`, `external → comfortable`). The density metrics are the control, row, surface and label metrics. The library owns them in `fuse.css`, mirrored by `DENSITY_METRICS` in `packages/fuse/src/theme/tokens/density-metrics.ts`: they are not in the token contract, themes never set them, and they ship in the standalone stylesheet. Subtree theming never rescopes them.
- **Control metric**: a length a control reads, such as `--control-h-md`: heights, inline paddings and gaps per control size (`xs`, `sm`, `md`, `lg`), plus the control type pair `--control-text` and `--control-leading`.
- **Density role**: which density metrics a part reads. Every `data-slot` the package writes declares one in `PART_DENSITY` (`packages/fuse/src/theme/tokens/density-roles.ts`): `control` (an interactive single-line box, reads control metrics), `row` (one line of a collection, reads row metrics), `surface` (a shell that pads content or a stack that spaces groups, reads surface metrics), `label` (the words that describe a control, or a notice's text beside the controls, reads label text), `layout` (sized by its children, its own padding and gap fixed) or `fixed` (identical at both densities). Where one slot renders with two roles, an override key, the slot followed by a CSS selector suffix such as `card[data-direction=horizontal]`, splits them; a container that freezes a part is a suffix too, such as `sidebar-group:where([data-collapsible=icon] *)` for the collapsed Sidebar icon rail. The docs density coverage test probes every rendered part at both densities: a part reads a density metric when one of its own properties answers a sentinel value of the metrics. A part with a metric role must read one, and a `layout` or `fixed` part must read none. The source contract requires every slot to be declared.
- **Row metric**: a length one line of a collection reads, such as a menu, listbox, navigation, sidebar or table row: `--row-h`, `--row-h-header` for a table head, `--row-px` and `--row-py`. A row keeps its text and the gaps between its own pieces fixed, so it grows only in height and padding, and a single-line row is exactly `--row-h`.
- **Surface gap**: the gap of a stack that spaces groups of parts, `--surface-gap-sm` to `--surface-gap-xl`. A gap of 6px or less between one part's own pieces, such as a label and its control in a FieldFrame, is part spacing and stays fixed.
- **Label text**: the type pair of the words that describe a control and of the text of a notice set beside the controls, `--label-text` and `--label-leading`, a step below the control text when comfortable. Field labels, titles, descriptions and errors, a label-variant legend, selection row titles and descriptions, and an Alert's title and body read it.
- **Surface metric**: a shell padding, `--surface-pad-sm`, `--surface-pad-md` or `--surface-pad-lg`, one per surface tier, or a surface gap. `--surface-pad-sm` is the same at both densities.
- **Surface tier**: the padding a shell takes, fixed per component by what the shell holds. `sm` pads shells of control-sized rows: menus, Select and Combobox lists, NavigationMenu content, the floating Sidebar's sections, Frame, Calendar and the date-picker presets. `md` pads compact content: Popover, Toast, Accordion items, the Field label card and Item's inline edge. `lg` pads content surfaces: Card sections and the gaps between them, Dialog, Frame panels and Empty (doubled from `md` up). The Command palette insets its input, rows and footer with the small surface gap, `--surface-gap-sm`, which is 8px dense and 12px comfortable, because no surface padding has those values. A shell pads with its tier's metric and subtracts the same metric from the inner corner it publishes. A row in an `sm` shell reads the row metrics.
- **Control size**: the size × fit mapping of control metrics onto a control. The sizes are `xs`, `sm`, `md` and `lg`. The fit says how the box fits its content: `label`, `min-square` or `square`. A `label` takes the size's height, gap, inline padding and type, and a leading or trailing `data-icon="inline-*"` child swaps that edge to the icon padding. Button's recipe swaps the inline padding and icon padding of its labels for its own, `--control-px-button-*` and `--control-px-button-icon-*`, which equal the control values when dense and, from `sm` up, widen to the customer-facing reference's padding when comfortable. A `min-square` is a label never narrower than it is tall, and a `square` is a fixed square of the control height. `xs` and `sm` use a fixed `text-xs`/`text-sm`, while `md` and `lg` use the density's `--control-text`/`--control-leading`. `packages/fuse/src/styles/control-size.ts` owns it, and controls without a size axis take the resolved md parts from `control-size-md.ts`. A control size is not a radius rung.
- **Theme**: a concrete permutation of variant × brand × segment, e.g. `internal-fkas-company`, `external-tkas-private`, `internal-elma-private`. 24 permutations exist (12 internal, 12 external).
- **Theme slug**: the canonical string name of a theme, `<variant>-<brand>-<segment>`.
- **Token / token contract**: a CSS custom property that components consume (e.g. a primary color role). The _contract_ is the fixed set of token names; themes vary values, never names. The library ships complete defaults; a theme overrides a subset.
- **Token kind**: what a contract token's value holds, which decides how exporters such as the Figma sync and the DTCG export translate it: `color`, `dimension` (a `rem` or `px` length), `fontFamily` (a font stack) or `fontWeight` (a plain number from 1 to 1000, such as `--selection-title-weight`). `TOKEN_KINDS` in `theme/tokens/contract.ts` gives every token one.
- **Radius rung**: a radius the library derives from a theme's `--radius` in whole multiples of the radius step, `--radius-xs` through `--radius-xl` plus `--radius-popover`. `--radius-md` is `--radius` minus one step. The internal variant steps 0px, so every internal rung equals `--radius`. Themes set only `--radius`, and the variant sets `--radius-step`.
- **Role token**: a semantic, themable token named for its job (`--primary`, `--card`, `--error`), following the shadcn grammar of base + `-foreground` pairs.
- **Primitive token**: a public but non-themed token holding a raw palette value — the neutral ramp (`--neutral-50..950`, 50 lightest) and the per-brand accents (`--brand-<code>`). Stable API, same values in every theme.
- **Soft form (`-soft`)**: the tinted-background companion of a role (`--error-soft`/`--error-soft-foreground`) — the contract's rename of Material-3's `-container` concept.
- **Derived role**: a contract token the pipeline computes from other composed roles instead of reading it from a layer, such as `--secondary-hover` (`secondary` mixed 5% toward `foreground`). A mixed role, such as `--secondary-hover`, is declared as a live `color-mix()` of its sources, so a host changes it by overriding those sources on the element that carries the theme attributes. A composed role, such as `--sidebar-brand`, is computed at build time: per theme, composition picks a literal color or an alias of a source such as `var(--brand)`. A literal ignores source overrides, so a host overrides the composed role itself. The catalog, the docs and design tools read the composed literal for both kinds. No palette may assign a derived role.
- **Radius step**: `--radius-step`, the spacing of the `rounded-*` scale around `--radius`, set once per variant. It also switches the corners in `packages/fuse/src/styles/corner-radius.ts` between the variants. `0px` means the internal variant, where every outer element rounds with the one radius and inner corners follow outer − inset. `2px` means the external variant, where those corners keep the reference's values. The formulas support no other value. A host changes the radius by overriding `--radius` on the element that carries the theme attributes, where the theme rules resolve `--radius-button` and `--radius-field`.
- **Inner corner**: the corner of an inner part, one laid against a shell's padding edge, such as a menu row, a tab or a field's addon button. It is concentric with the shell: `max(0px, outer − inset)`, where the inset is the shell's padding plus border on the axis that reaches the corner. The shell publishes it as `--inner-corner` on its padded element, and the part rounds with the public `rounded-inner` utility, which falls back to `--radius` outside a shell. Free-standing controls are outer elements and keep their rung. `packages/fuse/src/styles/corner-radius.ts` owns the contract.
- **Field radius**: `--radius-field`, the corner of every field box (Input, Textarea, Select, NumberField, InputGroup, Combobox chips and the React Aria field group), an alias of `var(--radius)` in every variant, so fields round with the brand radius like cards and selection rows. Each theme rule resolves it on the theme element, as it does the button radius, so a host can still set its own field corner there. Internal fields read `--radius` itself, so they follow a wrapper override as cards do; external fields follow the field radius the theme element resolved. The parts inside a field box round with its corner less their inset, as inner corners.
- **Feature role**: the strong brand-colored panel role (`--feature`) for promo/hero surfaces — deliberately distinct from `--accent`, which stays a subtle hover tint.
- **Must-override token**: a contract token a composed theme is required to supply through its non-default layers rather than inherit silently. The set is variant-specific: external themes supply their brand/surface/interactive/shape identity; internal themes supply the brand pair while inheriting the neutral system. It is a theme-level obligation, never a requirement on each layer module.
- **Interim tier (react-aria)**: components still built on `react-aria-components` (date/calendar family and a few atoms) pending a base-ui equivalent; part of the library, marked for future migration.
- **Supported locale**: a language the library ships built-in strings for — `nb-NO`, `sv-SE`, `en-US`, `fi-FI` at v1. Apps set one locale on the UI provider; components never take a locale directly.
- **Market time zone**: the IANA zone of the market a page's data describes, such as `Europe/Oslo` for Norwegian spot prices and meter readings. It is never the viewer's zone. The host passes it to `LocaleProvider` as `timeZone` beside the locale, so the server and every browser format dates on the market's clock. That removes the hydration differences that runtime zones cause. WebKit's date-time join still differs for some styles, as `data-table-format.ts` and `TODO.md` record. `DataTable` date cells read it, and a cell's own `timeZone` wins.
- **Slot**: one period of a time axis, named by its start instant and its interval: minute, quarter-hour, hour, day, week, month or year. A slot's range label ends at its wall-clock start plus the interval, never at an end instant, so the day's last hour reads `23–24` and the axis end tick reads `00`.
- **Repeated hour**: the wall-clock hour a fall-back day shows twice, 02:00 in Oslo and Stockholm and 03:00 in Helsinki. Tooltips, aria text and tables mark each of its slots with the CLDR short zone name, such as `02–03 (CEST)` and then `02–03 (CET)`, and ticks never do.
- **Canonical spaces**: every string Fuse formats for a chart writes each space as U+00A0, because Node and the browser engines disagree on which space Intl writes, and one differing space fails hydration. Consumer strings stay verbatim.
- **Spoken text**: the form of a chart label a speech engine reads, kept apart from the visual text in a `FormattedText`. Signs, units and ranges become words, such as `minus`, `kilowattimer` and `kl. 00 til 01`. `packages/fuse/src/components/chart/format/` owns both forms and the four-locale chart words.
- **String dictionary**: a component's built-in per-locale strings (empty states, nav labels, close buttons). Provides the correct-language default; an explicit string prop always overrides it.
- **State face**: a whole interactive control's disabled and invalid looks, plus the gate that keeps hover and press from repainting it while disabled. The disabled look is `opacity-50` with the `not-allowed` cursor. The invalid look is an `--error` border with a 3px ring of `--error` at 20% alpha. A keyboard-focused invalid control draws a solid 1px `--error` line between its border and that halo, which stays the outermost line. The gate is the `enabled-hover:`/`enabled-active:` variants, which negate the one `disabled-state` predicate in `fuse.css`: `:disabled`, `data-disabled` or `aria-disabled="true"`. The gate is the only mechanism, so a disabled control keeps pointer events and a Tooltip on it still opens. `packages/fuse/src/styles/state-face.ts` owns it as fixed class constants, one target per place a control reads its state (`native`, `data`, `aria`, `rac`, `within`). The `within` target reads only the group's own control, a direct child, so a control nested in another owner never paints the outer group. Parts such as calendar cells, date segments and labels are not state faces and keep their muted text colour.
- **Chart role**: the contract color of a chart mark, shared by every brand. The **categorical slots** `--chart-1` to `--chart-8` give series their identity in a fixed cyclic order, so a chart may start at any slot. The **sequential ramp** `--chart-seq-1` to `--chart-seq-7` encodes magnitude in one hue, step 1 the least. The **diverging scale** `--chart-diverging-1` to `--chart-diverging-7` encodes values that cross zero, with step 4 the grey midpoint at zero. A **sentiment role**, `--chart-sentiment-positive` or `--chart-sentiment-negative`, means favourable or unfavourable to the reader, such as a low or a high price, never the sign of a value. A chart uses sentiment roles or categorical slots, never both. **Other**, `--chart-other`, is the grey of the series folded together past the slot count. An **on-color role** such as `--chart-1-foreground` is the derived text color of a direct label on that slot or on Other. `chart-palette.test.ts` holds every chart role to the dataviz method's gates on each theme.
- **Text-grade role**: a token pairing whose foreground must meet 4.5:1 contrast on its surface in every theme. `feature-foreground` is not text-grade — it is accent/decorative; text on feature panels is white.
- **Phone editor**: the pure module behind `PhoneNumberField` that owns every change to the typed number, its country and the caret: edits, pastes, country picks, form reset and new props. An edit becomes a **proposal**, which carries its display, its output value and the caret, kept as the count of digits after each end of the selection. A proposal stays visible while a controlled parent echoes its output. Its caret stays when the parent's first answer shows the proposed display, whatever value the parent stores, and is dropped otherwise. The field and its hook only wire the editor to React and the DOM. `packages/fuse/src/components/phone-number-field/phone-editor.ts` owns it.
